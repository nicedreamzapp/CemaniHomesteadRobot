## Steering plate holes for the collar (laser shop does them, no drilling)
- Center hole for the 3/4 pin: 19.35 mm (as in the DXF).
- 2 x M6 clearance holes (6.6 mm), 180 deg apart, 14.73 mm from the center (29.46 mm apart), countersunk on the bottom face for M6 flat heads (the black plate sits right under).
- Torque and lift path: plate -> 2 M6 -> collar (clamped on the pin) -> pin -> bearing set screws.
- Stack: bearing inner ring 33 + collar 15 + plate 6.35 = 54.35 mm; the 2 in pin ends 3.5 mm above the plate bottom (the M6 screws locate the plate).
- The DXFs still show the old M5 collar holes at 14.3 mm: update them to the above before ordering. Caliper the collar first.
